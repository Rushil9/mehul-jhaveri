Bonds & fixed income

Understand government and corporate bonds, coupon payments, maturity and the issuer’s ability to repay. Compare yield at the purchase price, rather than coupon alone.

Bond prices can fall. Credit risk, interest-rate changes and limited buyers can affect repayment or an early sale; a fixed coupon is not a guaranteed total return.

Ask these four questions first

  1. What is the rate? Annual coupon, deposit interest or an indicative yield?
  2. Who pays it? Understand the issuer and repayment risk.
  3. When can I exit? Check lock-ins, notice periods, settlement and availability of buyers.
  4. What do I keep? Account for fees, penalties and taxes.

Rates and access depend on the specific product. Ask for current issuer documents and written exit terms before relying on a quoted return or withdrawal timeline.

MORE INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITIES

Explore specialist strategies.
Start with suitability.

PMS, SIF and AIF are different structures—not interchangeable upgrades to a mutual fund. Start with your experience, investment capacity and ability to accept losses or restricted access.

PMS

Portfolio Management Services

A professionally managed portfolio under an agreed mandate. Discuss the manager’s approach, holdings, concentration, turnover and reporting.

Review minimum investment and eligibility, management and performance fees, tax implications and exit terms. Returns are market-linked and losses are possible.

Start a PMS conversation →
SIF

Specialized Investment Funds

A framework for more flexible investment strategies within the mutual fund ecosystem. Understand the strategy, its use of derivatives and how its risks differ from a conventional fund.

Check the applicable aggregate investment threshold, eligibility, redemption frequency and strategy documents. Liquidity varies and returns are not assured.

Explore the SIF framework →
AIF

Alternative Investment Funds

Privately pooled investments following a defined strategy, which may include private equity, private credit or other alternatives depending on the fund category.

Review eligibility, capital commitments, drawdowns, fees, valuations and lock-ins. Some strategies can tie up money for years and carry substantial risk.

Understand AIF options →

These are educational introductions and invitations to discuss suitability, not product offers. Availability, eligibility, minimum investment and access must be confirmed with the relevant registered provider and current product documents.

Ask your own question ↗
How does a first meeting work?

We start by understanding your goals, time horizon, existing investments and comfort with risk. A portfolio review can then explore allocation, overlap, costs and how your investments fit your priorities. Use the contact form to request a discovery conversation.

Can I access my personal portfolio or transact here?

This public Investor Corner is for market information, learning and planning illustrations. For private portfolio access, statements, reporting or transaction assistance, contact our team to discuss the appropriate client-service channels.

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan is a way to invest a fixed amount regularly in a mutual fund. It supports discipline, but it does not guarantee profit or protect you from losses.

Why can my fund move differently from the Nifty?

A fund may hold different companies, sectors, market-cap sizes, cash or debt. Its expenses and investment strategy also affect returns. Compare it with an appropriate benchmark over a suitable period.

Does a higher NAV mean a fund is expensive?

No. NAV is the value of one unit after liabilities. A fund with a lower NAV is not automatically cheaper or better. Compare the portfolio, risks, costs and suitability instead.

Should I choose last year’s best-performing fund?

A strong year alone does not establish suitability. Consider consistency, downside risk, portfolio overlap, expenses and your goal. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

What does inflation do to my goal?

Inflation can raise the future cost of education, travel or other goals. The goal calculator estimates that cost using your assumption. Actual inflation may be different, especially for specific expenses.

Is the model index a product I can invest in?

No. It is an illustrative research basket maintained by the scheduled data refresh. It includes equity, hybrid and debt categories and is not an exchange index, a mutual fund or a recommended allocation.

How current are these market numbers?

This website displays dated snapshots refreshed on GitHub at approximately 07:10, 16:40 and 23:10 IST daily. It does not stream prices. “Check for a newer snapshot” retrieves the latest published file, and the page warns when data is older. The model basket can also check daily NAVs directly in your browser within its published review month. Model-review dates can differ from NAV dates.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required. Calculator entries are processed in your browser and are not saved by this site. A review request opens your email app, where you can review and send the message yourself.
